Our Story

About the Firm

Over three decades of principled advocacy, built on a foundation of integrity, expertise, and an unwavering commitment to justice.

Who We Are

A legacy built on
principle & results

Halloway & Partners was founded in Washington D.C. in 1994 by Richard Halloway, a former federal prosecutor with a vision: that every client — regardless of the complexity of their matter — deserves legal representation of the highest calibre.

From a two-attorney boutique, the firm has grown to a full-service practice of over twenty lawyers, united by a shared commitment to excellence, discretion, and client-first counsel.

Today, Halloway & Partners is recognised by peers and clients alike as one of the Washington region's most trusted legal institutions — a distinction earned through decades of results, not reputation alone.

"The measure of a great law firm is not only the cases it wins — but the clients it never abandons."
— Richard Halloway, Founding Partner

Halloway & Partners is a member of the American Bar Association, the D.C. Bar Association, and has been recognised by U.S. News & World Report as a Tier 1 law firm in Washington D.C. for eleven consecutive years.

Our History

Three decades of distinction

1994

Founding of Halloway & Partners

Richard Halloway, following a distinguished career as a federal prosecutor, establishes the firm in Washington D.C. with a focus on criminal defence and corporate law.

2001

Expansion into Family & Immigration Law

The firm welcomes Laura Reyes and Marcus Okafor as partners, broadening its practice into family law and immigration — two areas of growing client need in the D.C. region.

2007

Recognised as Tier 1 by U.S. News & World Report

For the first time, Halloway & Partners receives national recognition as a top-tier law firm in three practice areas — a distinction the firm has maintained ever since.

2013

Launch of Personal Injury Division

Senior Partner Sylvia Chen leads the formation of a dedicated personal injury practice, introducing the firm's contingency-fee model and expanding access to justice for individual clients.

2020

Opening of Second Office

A second office opens in Arlington, Virginia, extending the firm's reach and allowing greater service to clients across the greater Washington metropolitan region.

2024

30th Anniversary

Halloway & Partners celebrates thirty years of practice with over 2,400 cases resolved, a twenty-attorney team, and a commitment to the next thirty years of principled legal service.
